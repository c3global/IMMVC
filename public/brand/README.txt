UPLOAD YOUR LOGO FILES HERE
============================

Drop your logo PNGs into this folder using GitHub's web UI:

1. From this folder view, click "Add file" → "Upload files"
2. Drag your logo files in, OR click "choose your files"
3. Use these EXACT filenames (case-sensitive):

   logo-color.png   ← Full-color logo for light/white backgrounds
   logo-white.png   ← White / knockout version for dark backgrounds
   logo-mark.png    ← (optional) icon-only swirl, no wordmark

4. Scroll down, leave commit message default, click "Commit changes"

That's it. The site is wired to pick these up automatically the
moment they're committed.

Notes:
- Transparent background, highest resolution you have
- SVG is even better than PNG if you have it (same filenames, just .svg)
- This README can be deleted once your logos are in place
